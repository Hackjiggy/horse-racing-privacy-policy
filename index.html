1. Introduction

Welcome to Grey Man Tips, also known as Horse Racing. This Android application provides horse-racing information, racecards, horse selections, tips, and racing results to help users follow racing events.

This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles information, how third-party services may process data, and what choices users may have regarding their privacy.

2. Information We Collect

The information handled by the app depends on the features and services enabled in the installed version. Where applicable, the app or its service providers may process:

  • Device and technical information: Device model, operating-system version, app version, crash reports, and diagnostic information.
  • Usage information: App interactions, performance information, and advertising activity where third-party services collect it.
  • Network information: Information needed to connect to online services and retrieve racing tips, racecards, results, or other content.
  • Advertising identifiers: Advertising IDs or similar identifiers that advertising providers may process if ads are displayed.

The app does not require users to create an account solely to view general racing tips and results, unless a feature in a particular version states otherwise.

3. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to:

  • Display racing tips, selections, racecards, and results.
  • Retrieve and update racing information from external data providers.
  • Maintain app functionality, stability, and performance.
  • Diagnose technical issues and help prevent abuse.
  • Display and measure advertisements, where applicable.
  • Improve the app and its features.

4. Horse-Racing Information and Disclaimer

The app may obtain horse-racing information from external providers or publicly available sources. We aim to provide useful and accurate information, but racecards, selections, predictions, and results may contain errors, delays, or incomplete information.

Tips and predictions are provided for informational and entertainment purposes only. They do not guarantee a winning outcome or financial return. Users are responsible for their own decisions and must comply with the laws and age restrictions applicable in their location.

Third-party providers are responsible for their own processing practices, as described in their privacy policies.

7. Data Storage and Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information handled by the app against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or disclosure. Racing information and preferences may be stored locally on the user's device. Online services, if used, may process information on their own systems.

No method of electronic storage or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Information stored locally may remain on the device until the user deletes it, clears the app's data, or uninstalls the app. Information processed by third-party providers may be retained under their policies and applicable legal requirements.

For requests concerning personal information handled by the developer, contact us using the details in the Contact section below.

9. Children's Privacy

The app is not intended to encourage children to participate in gambling. Users must comply with applicable age restrictions and laws in their country.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through a dedicated account-registration process. If you believe personal information has been collected inappropriately, please contact the developer.

The app may rely on external websites, racing-data providers, or other third-party services. These services operate independently and may have their own privacy policies and terms. We encourage users to review those policies when using external services.
